A newspaper front page summing up Tuesday, 24 November 2026 in one image:
36 dated stories across 9 regions — Huawei convicted on 10 of 12 counts
in a US court, the IRGC attacks a ship in the Strait of Hormuz while
four tankers hit mines, a suspected Houthi drone hits the Riyadh PIF
tower, and the Houthis advance to within 60 km of Aden. Every story
happened, or was first reported, on 24 November 2026 (UTC); the
DATE CHECK box lists the neighbouring days' big news that was left out.

`main.ts` copies the front page (`entry.png`, 1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to the
grader's output directory as `entry.png`. The page was composed in HTML/CSS
and every illustration (including the world map with numbered pins) was drawn
in code — no photos.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```
